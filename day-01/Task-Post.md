💡 Key Takeaways from Day 1:

• What is Cloud Computing? 
Renting computing resources like servers, databases, and storage over the internet – pay only for what you use, no maintenance.

• Cloud vs Traditional IT: 
Flexible, auto-scaling, and managed by providers | vs. | costly, fixed-capacity infrastructure you maintain yourself.

• Why Choose GCP?
 - Same infrastructure powering YouTube & Gmail
 - Strong AI & networking capabilities
 - User-friendly console & CLI
 - Cost-effective & beginner-friendly

• Types of Cloud:
 - Public: Shared by everyone (e.g., Google Drive, AWS)
 - Private: Exclusive to one organization
 - Hybrid: Best of both worlds

• Essential Cloud Terms: Virtualization, VM, API, Regions, Availability Zones, Scalability, Elasticity, Agility, High Availability, Fault Tolerance, Disaster Recovery, Load Balancing.
